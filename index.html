What I noticed, and what I would do about it

These are outside-in observations. I can only see part of the picture from here, and the honest use of that is to show you how I look at an account, not to pretend I know what is inside yours.

cbdMDCBD · Wellness

CBD is a restricted category on both Google and Meta. That one fact shapes everything about how this account should be run, and it is where generic e-commerce playbooks quietly fall apart.

Worth a lookThree separate GA4 properties load on the storefront. Three properties usually means three versions of the truth. If reporting on this account ever feels slightly off, this is the first place I would check.
Worth a lookThe Meta pixel stayed silent on my first visit. That could be consent settings doing their job, or it could be a gap. Either way, it decides whether Meta is running on a full signal or a partial one.
Working for youKlaviyo and Microsoft Clarity are in place. Retention and behavior data exist here, which is more than most CBD brands ever set up properly.

With access, week one goes to measurement: one source of truth in GA4, pixel and consent behavior verified event by event, and only then the restricted-category paid plan on top of clean data.

Wolf MattressDTC · Home

An American mattress maker with roots back to 1873 is a story most paid accounts would kill for. From the outside, paid media barely seems to use it.

Worth a lookThe only search ad I could find is a branded one. Branded clicks mostly harvest demand the brand already earned. The growth lives in non-branded search and Shopping, and that lane looks quiet.
Worth a lookI did not see a Google Ads conversion tag or a Meta pixel on a first visit. If that holds up inside the account, any spend is being optimized on incomplete signals.
Working for youGTM is installed and GA4 is a single, clean property. Someone laid a decent foundation, which makes fixing the rest faster.

With access, week one: confirm what the conversion tags actually record, then map the non-branded and Shopping opportunity against margin, not against vanity ROAS.

Everything above is visible from outside the accounts. Where I could not verify something, I flagged it as a question instead of dressing a guess up as a finding. You would get the same honesty about your clients' accounts every week.
